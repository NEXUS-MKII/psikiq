01The catalogue+
138 trades resolve to 25 verticals. What you do decides which instruments get fitted — a plumber and a law firm are not read with the same ruler.
02Universal markers+
Twelve questions read developmental stage independent of trade. Does structure exist outside your head. Do decisions escalate. Does delivery run without you. Stage isn’t size — these are what separate them.
03The sector overlay+
Where a sector model exists, three more read tooling maturity in that trade’s own language — workpapers, matter management, quoting discipline. Not a generic question with the noun swapped.
04The comparator+
The two reads are scored separately, then cross-validated against each other. Agreement raises confidence. Disagreement is not noise — it is the finding.
05Divergence+
When general maturity outruns the tooling beneath it, that gap is the crisis already forming. The engine names it, sizes it, and flags the business as at the edge — the moment it is cheapest to act.

Every read returns its own confidence, and says so when it can only give you the coarse one.
